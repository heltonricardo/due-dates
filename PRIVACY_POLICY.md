# Privacy Policy — Due Dates

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026

Due Dates ("the app") is a reminder app for recurring and one-time due dates. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## Data the app stores

The app stores the reminders you create (titles, notes, dates, recurrence rules, icons, colors, completion status) and your settings (notification time, theme color). This data is saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

## Data the app does not collect

- No account or sign-in is required.
- No analytics, crash reporting, or advertising tools are included.
- No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
- The app does not request internet access and does not communicate with any server.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** used only to show your reminders on the days and time you choose.

## Backups

- **Manual export/import:** if you use Export, the app writes a JSON file to the location you choose. You control where that file is stored and who can access it. Import reads a file you select.
- **Android backup:** if automatic backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the app's data in your Google backup. This is handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. The app's developer has no access to it.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes its data from your device. Files you exported manually must be deleted by you.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new effective date.
